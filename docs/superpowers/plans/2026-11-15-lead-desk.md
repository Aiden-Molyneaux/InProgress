**Goal:** with Grok closed, the window answers "where are we" for a project: a Lead view that shows the lead's
brief, regrouped for a human, beside the lead's log; and a project action that gives the project a lead or takes it
away.

The same facts as the brief, regrouped the way the lead's short answer goes (what needs you, what is moving, what

## Criteria (depth for Aiden: a list, on the check surface)

- **C1** In a project's actions choose `Lead…`, type a name, Enter: the Lead view's head names that lead. Open it
  again, clear the field, Enter: the view reads `No lead. Give this project one from its actions.`
- **C2** Click Lead in the rail (code `LED`): the view shows, in this order, Goal and Phase, Needs you, Moving,
  Stuck, Next (with the backlog count), The lead's promises, Landed, Said in your name, and the Links line. Each
  ask under Needs you is whole, with its kind, its step and its age; your own active steps lead that list.
- **C3** Beside it, the lead's log, newest first: time, kind, the step or ask, the text, and your words in quotes
  when the line carries them.
- **C4** Change the record elsewhere (clear an ask in another window): the view shows it within two seconds,
  without a reload.
- **C5** An empty group reads `none`. A project with no lead still shows the brief, under the no-lead line.
- **C6** Opening the view never marks the brief as read: the lead's "since your last look" does not move.
- **C7** On a copy of the live data, the view shows the brief within one second of the click.

## Decisions

1. **Two columns:** the desk (the brief regrouped) on the left, the lead's log on the right; one column under 900 px,
   the log last. Groups, in order: Needs you, Moving, Stuck, Next, The lead's promises, Landed, Said in your name.
   Every group is always shown; an empty one reads `none`. No list is cut: the page scrolls.
2. **The grouping and every word of it live in a pure module,** `web/src/lead/leadDesk.ts`, tested without React.
   The component only lays out what the module returns.
3. **Ages count to the window's clock** (`now`, as the other views get it), not to the brief's `at`.
4. **Loading:** only while the view is open: when it opens, when the project changes, and each time the snapshot
   object changes (the window refreshes it on every stream event for the project, which is the live update). A
   sequence number keeps an older answer from landing over a newer one; a failed load keeps the last brief and
   shows the error line.
5. **A row that names a step opens it,** as rows in the other views do (`onOpenNode`).
6. **`Lead…` reuses the menu's inline field** (the one `Rename…` uses), prefilled with the current lead. Enter with
   a name sets it, Enter with an empty field takes the lead away, the same name closes with nothing to save, Escape
   and leaving cancel. Shown on an active project's row only.
7. **`spanAgo` and `stamp` move to `src/core/span.ts`** so the window can use them; `src/server/briefing.ts` and
   `src/server/digest.ts` re-export them, so no other import changes. `LeadEventOut` moves to `src/core/lead.ts`
   the same way.
8. No rail figure for Lead. No new stream event. No change to any `/api/lead/` route.

## Task 1: the desk's words (`leadDesk.ts`) and its test

`span` is `since the lead's last look` when `b.links.lastSeenAt` is set, else `in the last 7 days`
(`LEAD_LOOK_BACK_DAYS`). `step` is `#<id> <title>`. Ages are `spanAgo(iso, now)`.

| Group | title | count | note | rows, in the brief's order |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `needs` | `Needs you` | own steps + asks | `Open asks across all projects: <m> of 5`, then `, <k> over the limit` when over (`AIDEN_OPEN_LIMIT`) | `aidensOwn` first: text `step`, meta `your own step, active · last changed <age> ago`. Then `waitingOnAiden`: text `a<id> <kind> · step`, detail the ask's text whole, meta `<age>` |
| `moving` | `Moving` | sessions | `<n> of 3 in flight` (`IN_FLIGHT_LIMIT`) | text `step`, or the row's title when it has no step; meta `<state> · <app> · seen <age> ago` |
| `stuck` | `Stuck` | stale steps | none | text `step`, detail the reasons joined with `; ` |
| `next` | `Next` | next up | `Backlog: <n> steps not started and not in the queue` (`step` for 1) | text `step`, meta the status |
| `promises` | `The lead's promises` | lead asks | none | text `a<id> · step`, detail the text, meta `due <stamp>` or `no due time`, `overdue` as given |
| `landed` | `Landed <span>` | landed steps | none | text `step`, detail the outcome when it has one |
| `said` | `Said in your name <span>` | cited words | none | text `<stamp> <kind> <ref>`, detail the words inside double quotes |

Every row that names a step carries its `nodeId`. `key` is unique within its group (the ask id, the step id, the
session id, or the index for `said`).

`deskHead`: `lead` and `instructions` from the brief (`b.project.lead`, `b.instructions.current`); `goal` is the
brief's, else `The intent record has no goal under a Goal heading.` when the record has been written
(`intent.updatedAt` set), else `No intent record yet.`; `phase` is the brief's, else `none`; `warning` is
`The last relay may not have reached its session (<age> ago).` under the rule `renderLeadBrief` uses
(`links.t3 === 'unavailable'`, `t3At` set, and no look on record or `t3At` after it), else null; `links` is
`T3 Code <t3>`, then `, last relay <age> ago` when `t3At` is set, then ` · the lead's last look <age> ago` or
` · the lead's last look never`.

`logLines`: one per event, in the order given. `at` is `stamp(e.at)`; `kind` is the event's kind with `_` as a
space; `ref` is `a<askId>` when the event names an ask, else `#<nodeId>` when it names a step, else `''`; `text` is
the event's text with whitespace runs as one space; `words` is `aidenWords`; `by` is `<lead> · v<instructions>`;
`nodeId` when the event has one.

Test `test/web/lead-desk.test.ts`, from one fixture brief with something in every list and one with nothing:
the seven group ids in order; each title, count and note exactly; own steps before asks; an ask of 600 characters
kept whole; `Backlog: 1 step` and `Backlog: 21 steps`; the two spans; an overdue promise; an empty brief gives seven
groups with no rows; `deskHead` for no lead, for each goal fallback, for the warning shown and not shown, and the
links text for `not checked`, `ok` and `unavailable`; `logLines` for an ask event with words, a step event, an
event with neither, and `relay_unknown` reading `relay unknown`.
