## Phase 0 facts (from the code at 0b1f21e)

- The route that sets a lead exists: `POST /api/ui/projects/:id/lead` (`src/server/routes/window.ts:90-94`), body
  `windowLeadBody` `{ slug, name: string 1 to 40 | null }`, calling `setProjectLead` (records `project.updated`).
  Nothing under `web/src` calls it, and there is no Lead view and no `/api/lead/` client in `web/src/api.ts`.
- The reads exist: `GET /api/lead/:ref/brief` without `as` is the window's (no lead check, never marks) and answers
  `{ brief: LeadBrief, text }`; `GET /api/lead/:ref/events` answers `{ events }`, newest first, at most 100.
- A view is a member of `View` and `VIEWS` (`web/src/views/view.ts:4,8`), a rail entry
  (`web/src/chrome/railLogic.ts:87-93`) and a branch of the render chain in `web/src/App.tsx:441-463`.
- `DocsView` gets its data from a hook that `App.tsx` runs (`useProjectDocs(slug, open)`), loaded when the slug
  changes and each time the view opens. Nothing polls. The window's stream handler is `web/src/useInProgress.ts:193-207`.
- Rename is an inline field in the project menu (`web/src/chrome/projectMenus.tsx`: `RenameField` 84-102, `RowMenu`
  271, the item at 332), saved through `App.tsx:256-261`, `useInProgress.ts:261-263`, `api.ts:105`.
- `test/web/` holds pure-logic tests only: no test renders a component. So the view's grouping and wording live in
  a pure module, and that is what the unit test covers.
- A relay writes only to `lead_events`, with no stream event. A delivered one is followed by the worker's own
  prompt event, which refreshes the view; a failed or unknown one shows at the next change or reopening.

## Criteria (depth for Aiden: a list, on the check surface)

- **C1** In a project's actions choose `Lead…`, type a name, Enter: the Lead view's head names that lead. Open it
  again, clear the field, Enter: the view reads `No lead. Give this project one from its actions.`
- **C2** Click Lead in the rail (code `LED`): the view shows, in this order, Goal and Phase, Needs you, Moving,
  Stuck, Next (with the backlog count), The lead's promises, Landed, Said in your name, and the Links line. Each
  ask under Needs you is whole, with its kind, its step and its age; your own active steps lead that list.
- **C3** Beside it, the lead's log, newest first: time, kind, the step or ask, the text, and your words in quotes
  when the line carries them.
- **C4** Change the record elsewhere (clear an ask in another window): the view shows it within two seconds,
  without a reload.
- **C5** An empty group reads `none`. A project with no lead still shows the brief, under the no-lead line.
- **C6** Opening the view never marks the brief as read: the lead's "since your last look" does not move.
- **C7** On a copy of the live data, the view shows the brief within one second of the click.
