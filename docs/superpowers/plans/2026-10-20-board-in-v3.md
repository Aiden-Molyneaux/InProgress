## Criteria (the bar for the e2e, Parvati and Aiden's list)

- **C1 head and slab.** At 1600: the code block, the name, the meta `N ASKS · N LIVE · N READY · N DONE` (a zero is
  dim). The slab shows the note's first sentence; a `#id` in it is a link that opens that step's Details. More opens the
  whole note as Markdown in place (its `#id`s link too), Less folds it. "N to tidy" opens a Menu of the marked steps; an
  item opens its Details; Escape closes the menu with focus back on the button. The No claimed step row shows when a
  live session is on no Now row.
- **C2 columns.** Page width 1160 and up: four columns Needs me, Next, Now, Done. 720 to 1159: two by two (Needs me,
  Now / Next, Done). Under 720: one column Needs me, Now, Next, Done. Each column head: glyph, name, Count, hint.
- **C3 Needs me.** Two asks on one step read as two panels, each with its own Answer… and Clear. Answer… becomes a Field
  and Send; Enter sends; Escape returns focus to Answer…. A sent or cleared ask strikes through until the snapshot drops
  it. A permission wait shows the command in a terminal readout and Open session to allow ↗. YOU is a needs Chip.
- **C4 Next and the preview.** Alt+Down on a focused Next tile moves it one place down (Alt+Up up) and focus stays on
  it; drag still reorders. Own queues sit under `// QUEUED BY <title>`. The preview has a phase TabStrip with counts,
  one-line rows, to Next on hover or focus, and "N more in <phase> · Open Backlog ›" past five.
- **C5 Now and Done.** A Now tile shows `Task n of m · <task>` and `▸ <newest command> · <age>` in a terminal readout,
  and Done… opens the confirm in the tile. Done tiles are compact, the violet since-ruler reads
  `SINCE YOU LOOKED · HH:MM`, and the foot is `All done · N ›`.
- **C6 every tile.** A click on a tile (not its controls) opens Details and the tile stays marked (volt edge) until
  Details closes. Arrows walk the tiles across and down the columns as one Tab stop; Enter opens Details; Tab reaches the
  tile's own buttons. On a project's first paint the tiles rise in a stagger; an ordinary snapshot animates nothing.

Owner check depth: a list (C1 to C6 as guided steps on the check surface).

## The DOM contract (tests and App rely on it; keep it exact)

- Each column is `<section class="ip-band ip-band-<needs|next|now|done>" aria-label="<Needs me|Next|Now|Done>">` with
  its head `<h2 tabIndex={-1}>` holding the name. App's ASKS focus becomes `.ip-band-needs h2`.
- Each tile is the Tile part's `<article>` inside an `<li>` of the column's `<ul|ol class="ip-list">`. The article
  carries `data-node={id}` (absent on a focusless permission box), `data-state={box.state}`, the cursor props, and a
  class per band: `ip-tile ip-needs-tile | ip-next-row | ip-now-row | ip-done-tile`. Its title button has class
  `ip-row-title` (via the new `titleClassName`), so `[data-node="N"] .ip-row-title` still finds it.
- Own controls keep `data-act` (a click on them never opens Details). Keep the hooks: `.ip-hover-act` on back to
  Backlog, to Next and Done…; `data-done` on Done…; `.ip-done-form` on the Done confirm; `data-ask={id}` on each ask
  panel; `data-session={id}` on a Now session's readout.
- Preview rows: `<li>` items of `<ul class="ip-list ip-backlog-list" aria-label="Backlog: <phase>">`, each the
  BacklogRow element with `class="ip-backlog-row"`, `data-node`, a title button `.ip-row-title`, draggable.
- `web/src/board/focus.ts`: the row selector also takes `article.ip-tile:not([data-pending])` and
  `.ip-backlog-row`; when the row found is a cursor item (`[data-cursor]`) focus the item itself, else its
  `.ip-row-title`; the home fallback looks for `h2` before `h3`.

## Shared decisions (every batch follows these)

- **Layout.** `Board` puts `usePageWidth` on its root and derives `layout`: `'four'` at 1160 and up, `'two'` from 720 to
  1159, `'one'` under 720 (a pure `layoutFor(width)` in `web/src/board/layout.ts`, unit-tested; width 0, before the
  first measure, counts as `'four'`). Column order: `'four'` is `WIDE_ORDER` (needs, next, now, done); `'two'` and
  `'one'` are `NARROW_ORDER` (needs, now, next, done). CSS: `.ip-board[data-layout=four] .ip-bands` is
  `grid-template-columns: 1.12fr 1fr 1.12fr 0.94fr` (each `minmax(0, …)`), gap `--sp-6`; `two` is two equal columns,
  row gap `--sp-section`; `one` is one column. `BandProps.wide` is `layout === 'four'`. Delete `useWide.ts` once nothing
  imports it (Backlog.tsx does not: check).
- **Column head** (`web/src/board/ColumnHead.tsx`): `<header class="ip-col-head">` with the Eyebrow-style glyph
  (`[!]`, `>>`, `[◈]`, `[✓]`), the `<h2 tabIndex={-1}>` name in the display face at `--t-h3`, a `Count` (`size="h3"`,
  `tone` volt for Needs me, muted for the rest) at the right, and under the name the hint in mono `--t-detail-sm`:
  "Oldest wait first", "Queue order, drag or Alt+arrows", "Live sessions, working first", `Latest of ${board.done.length}`.
  A hairline under the head: `--volt` for Needs me (glyph volt too), `--line` for the rest. The Count is the number of
  tiles in the column (Done: `board.done.length`).
- **Hue.** `hueOf(box): number | null` in `boxState.ts`, the same rule as `hueStyle` (coloured states only, a valid
  index); the Tile gets `hue={hueOf(box)}`. Done tiles and preview rows pass none.
- **Selected.** `ViewProps.selectedNode?: number | null`; App passes its `selectedNode`. A tile is
  `selected={selectedNode === node.id}`.
- **The keyboard grid.** One `useListCursor` in `Board` over the four columns in the layout's column order. `counts`
  and `keys` come from what each column draws, in draw order: Needs me `boxes.needs` (key `n<id>` or `s<sessionId>`),
  Next the shown order from `useNextQueue` (below), Now `boxes.now`, Done the full (unfolded) tiles shown. `onOpen` opens
  that tile's Details (no-op for a focusless box). Each column gets `cursor: (row: number) => CursorItemProps` for its
  index and spreads it on the Tile; a Tile that also needs its own `onKeyDown` (Next, for Alt+arrows) calls the cursor's
  handler first and then its own. The Backlog preview's rows are a second `useListCursor` (one column) inside
  PreviewPanel.
- **Next's order is lifted** into `web/src/board/useNextQueue.ts`, called in `Board`: it holds today's `pending`
  optimistic order, the `boxOf` map, `next`, `unowned`, `owned` groups, `backlog`, and `write`, `toNext`, `toBacklog`,
  moved verbatim from `NextColumn.tsx:28-97`. NextColumn takes its result as a prop and keeps the drag state.
- **Alt+Up / Alt+Down** on a focused unowned Next tile (the article, not a control in it): Alt+Up writes
  `dropToOp(id, { zone: 'next', before: <id of the unowned tile above it> })`; Alt+Down writes `before: <id of the tile
  two below>` or `null` when it would be last. At either end, and on own-queue tiles, nothing happens. Prevent the
  default only when a write goes out. Focus stays on the tile (its element is keyed by node id). `noteAction('next',
  'key_move')`.
- **Rise.** `web/src/board/useRise.ts`: a pure `riseFor(prev, keys, at)` plus a hook. On the Board's first render every
  tile key (`<band>:<id>`) gets its stagger place (grid order, capped at 11); later a key not seen in the previous
  render gets 0 (it rises alone); a key keeps its rise for 900 ms, then `undefined`. Pass it as the Tile's `rise`.
  Reduced motion is the parts layer's job (check `parts.css` does it; if not, say so, do not add it).
- **Session line** (`web/src/board/SessionLine.tsx`, replacing TaskBox's for the Board; reuse `OpenScope`,
  `OpenButton`, `OpenFailure` from `TaskBox.tsx` by import): a diamond `◆` in `var(--p-hue, var(--text-dim))`, the
  session's display title (ellipsis), then its state word (`WORDS[sessionActivity(s, now)]` from `web/src/live`) or the
  role (`drives` / `helping`) on Now, then **Open ↗** (Button ghost sm, glyph `↗`, "Opening…" while busy). Under it
  `OpenFailure`.
- **Foot** (`web/src/board/TileFoot.tsx`): the handoff as a `<button data-act class="ip-tile-handoff">` holding a quiet
  Chip `handoff · <age>` (opens the viewer, `noteAction(area, 'open_handoff')`), `▤ n` mono muted for documents, the
  label as a `label` Chip (glyph and word, from `LABEL_GLYPHS`), hygiene words (`MARK_WORDS`, less `ended_open`) as
  quiet Chips. YOU (`node.assignee === 'me'`, not done) is a `needs` Chip in the tile's top chips, not the foot.
- **Empty columns** use `EmptyState` with the spec's words: "Nothing needs you", "Nothing is queued", "Nothing is in
  flight", "Nothing done yet", and "Nothing in <phase>" in the preview.
- **Words.** No em dashes anywhere (`test/repo/no-em-dash.test.ts`).
